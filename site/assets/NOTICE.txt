Third-party material in the distfiles.gentoozh.org site
(gentoo-zh/binhost, site/)

Design
The site's documentation layout follows the documentation of Adobe's React
Spectrum and Spectrum 2 (https://react-spectrum.adobe.com/). Neutral and
status colours, spacing, radii and type sizes are values from
@adobe/spectrum-tokens 15.4.1 (Apache License 2.0,
https://github.com/adobe/spectrum-design-data); /assets/tokens.css names
the token each value comes from. The licence text is
/assets/LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt. The accent is Gentoo's purple from the
tyrian theme (https://gitweb.gentoo.org/sites/tyrian-theme.git).

OSU Open Source Lab logo
The logo on /thanks is the Oregon State University Open Source Lab's own
artwork: /assets/osuosl-logo-light.png is
https://osuosl.org/images/osllogo-print_0.png and
/assets/osuosl-logo-dark.png is https://osuosl.org/images/osllogo-white_0.png,
both retrieved 2026-09-29 and unmodified. The page only scales them. OSUOSL's
logo rules (https://osuosl.org/about/logos/) allow resizing but no change to
colours or alignment.

Mirror logos
Each mirror's logo on /thanks is that mirror's own artwork, taken from the
logo its website shows, retrieved 2026-09-29. The page only scales them and
never changes their colours; a logo that cannot be read on the dark theme
sits on a white tile there instead.
- /assets/logos/cernet.svg: https://mirrors.cernet.edu.cn/static/img/mirrorz.svg
  (the MirrorZ logo in the site's sidebar). Inkscape and RDF metadata and
  the title removed; shapes and colours unchanged.
- /assets/logos/nju.webp: https://mirror.nju.edu.cn/assets/nju-CWbhqkwx.png
  (the site's header logo), scaled from 400x400 to 128x128, lossless WebP.
- /assets/logos/nyist.webp: https://mirror.nyist.edu.cn/static/img/logo-share.png
  (the site's header logo), its transparent margin trimmed (to 336x333),
  then scaled to 129x128, lossless WebP.
- /assets/logos/hernet.svg: https://mirrors.ha.edu.cn/static/img/logo-hernet.svg
  (the HERNET mark in the site's header), unchanged.
